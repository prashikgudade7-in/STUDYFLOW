Cookies and browser storage

Technical review date: 19 November 2026

Draft — operator and legal review required. This inventory reflects the application source reviewed on this date. Hosting infrastructure and third-party provider behavior may add processing that is not visible in this repository.

2. Other browser storage

StudyFlow uses localStorage key studyflow_ai_pref to remember the selected AI provider, model, and optional Azure endpoint. It does not store the API key. This value remains until cleared by the user in browser settings or site-data controls. It is browser storage, not a cookie.

3. Analytics and optional tracking

No analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking pixel, or optional tracking cookie was identified in the reviewed application source. No cookie-consent manager is present. A consent banner has not been added because this review found no optional tracking cookies to control; this is not a legal determination that consent is unnecessary for every deployment or data use. Reassess before adding analytics, advertising, or other nonessential tracking.

4. Fonts

DM Sans and Manrope are served from the StudyFlow site. Their SIL Open Font License is included in the project. The current frontend does not make a Google Fonts request.

5. Changing your choices

You may clear StudyFlow cookies and localStorage through your browser's site-data settings. Signing out invalidates the current server session and clears its cookie. Clearing localStorage removes the remembered AI provider preferences. Blocking the required session cookie prevents authenticated features from working.
