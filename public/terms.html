Terms of use

Technical review date: 19 November 2026

Draft — not ready for public launch. The operator identity, eligibility rules, service commitments, complaint channel, governing-law position, and legally reviewed contract terms have not been supplied. This page is not a final agreement and does not establish a legal relationship.

1. Operator and eligibility

The repository does not identify the StudyFlow operator or a contact address. The owner's intended minimum age is 18; users under 18 may use the service only with parent/guardian consent and controls. The current build does not implement age verification or guardian approval, so it does not enforce this policy. The owner must implement and legally review these controls before inviting users under 18.

2. Accounts and security

Users should provide accurate account information and protect their sign-in credentials. StudyFlow uses an authentication cookie to maintain a session. Signed-in users can export their profile, study records, and completed challenge history as JSON, or permanently delete their account and associated active-database records by re-entering their password in Settings. Deletion does not itself remove deployment backups or information already processed by third-party providers.

3. Study material and acceptable use

Users should only submit material they are authorized to use and should avoid entering sensitive personal information. Nothing in this draft transfers ownership of user-created material. Any license needed to host, display, back up, or process user material must be defined by the operator and reviewed by counsel before these terms are used.

Users must not use the service to violate another person's rights, interfere with service operation, or attempt unauthorized access. The owner must confirm how reports are received and how these rules are enforced.

4. AI and third-party services

Student-data AI requests are blocked unless the selected provider has documented approval for text, images, and documents. Provider configuration remains available, and a connection test sends only a fixed, non-student test prompt plus the user's selected API key; user-supplied prompts, images, and documents are rejected from that test route. No provider is currently approved for student-data processing. If approvals are recorded later, provider terms, retention, output quality, and availability remain subject to that provider's terms. Verify generated explanations before relying on them. StudyFlow's study content is not a substitute for a teacher, school policy, or professional advice.

5. Availability, fees, and legal terms

The repository describes a local StudyFlow build and does not establish public-service availability, support levels, pricing, payment, refunds, warranty terms, liability allocation, dispute handling, or governing law. The operator must decide and have counsel review these terms rather than rely on assumptions in this draft. Mandatory consumer or other non-waivable rights are not addressed or waived here.

6. Intellectual property and reports

The application identifies AI integrations using text labels rather than provider logo images. Provider names may be trademarks of their respective owners; this draft does not claim affiliation or endorsement. A contact channel for rights complaints is not configured, and the owner must publish an operational reporting process before launch.

7. Privacy and changes

Read the draft Privacy notice and draft Cookie notice. Those pages also require owner input and legal review. The operator must determine how terms are updated and how affected users are notified; no such process is established by this draft.

8. Contact

No operator or support contact is configured in the project. This page must not be presented as final until the owner supplies those details and obtains qualified legal review.
